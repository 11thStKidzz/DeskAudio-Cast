桌面声渡 / DeskAudio Cast 隐私政策
适用应用版本：0.1.13。更新日期：2026-10-28。
发布者：11thStKidzz
隐私和用户支持邮箱：woyaotingspotify@gmail.com

1. 适用范围
本政策说明本应用访问、处理、传输及保留哪些信息。没有服务器上传，不等于应用不处理个人信息。系统声音可能包含私人内容，设备名称、网络地址和设备标识也可能关联个人或家庭。本政策不替代 Windows、Microsoft Store、WebView2 或你主动使用的邮件服务的政策。

2. 系统声音
你开始投送，或主动开启自动投送后，本应用使用 Windows WASAPI 回环读取所选输出设备正在播放的系统声音，可能包括通知和私人内容。声音在内存中按当前传输格式处理、ALAC 编码后，通过网络发送到你选择的接收设备。应用不主动采集麦克风，不把捕获的原始或编码音频保存到文件，也没有应用自有云端音频上传服务。停止投送可停止这一路声音传输；不要投送无权播放或分享的内容。

3. 设备发现、网络与配对
应用通过 mDNS 在可互通的网络中发现设备，处理设备名称、IP 地址、端口、设备标识及协议能力，并与所选设备交换连接、控制及播放数据。设备标识可能来源于设备公布的 MAC 形式标识；它不是访问互联网网站或广告跟踪的用途。
当前连接边界接受私有或本地链路地址，以及通过发现获得的 IPv6 全球单播路由；手工输入不接受公网地址。私有地址可能经 VPN 路由，不能保证一定处于同一物理局域网。请只连接可信网络和设备；协议加密不是对全部攻击的保证。当前配对身份仅驻留内存，明文持久化身份入口已禁用。旧版独立工具产生的身份文件不会被自动删除。

4. 本地偏好和自动功能
为恢复使用体验，应用在逻辑路径 %APPDATA%/com.personal.desktopaudiotransmitter/settings.json 保存上次接收设备的名称、地址、端口和标识、各设备偏好、选定声音输出标识、音量、模式、语言及功能开关。语言也保存在应用 WebView 本地存储中。设置没有自动过期时间，直到你清除或更改。
开机启动和自动投送为可选功能，默认关闭。启用自动投送后，后台会寻找上次设备，并可能把系统中正在播放的所有声音发送给该设备，沿用保存的音量和模式。可关闭此选项；手动停止后，本次运行不会自动恢复，重新开启可恢复。开机启动还受 Windows 启动应用设置控制。

5. 日志与主动导出的诊断
当前日志仅允许数字性能指标，不记录音频样本、编码字节、设备名称、IP、配对密钥或任意原始错误。逻辑日志目录为 %APPDATA%/ConexionAirPlay/logs，此旧目录名为兼容而保留。
应用在启动及每六小时尽力清理本软件的每日旧日志，目标为 14 天、最多 14 份。最近 24 小时或被占用的文件会跳过，故可临时超出；不跟随目录链接，不清理其他文件。较早版本日志可能含敏感内容。
你主动导出的诊断 JSON 位于逻辑路径 %LOCALAPPDATA%/com.personal.desktopaudiotransmitter/diagnostics。包含版本、构建指纹、会话状态、计数和耗时；每个输出最多保留最近 64 次数字欠载事件及回退判断信息。不附原日志，不含声音、设备名称、地址或密钥。事件在导出前仅驻留内存；导出文件保留至你主动删除，不由日志清理功能删除。导出本身不上传，分享前请自行查看。

6. 发布者及第三方接收信息
应用没有自己的账号、广告或自动业务遥测服务，也不会自动向上述支持邮箱发送信息。声音及必要控制数据发送到你选择的接收设备。Windows、WebView2 和 Microsoft Store 可能按各自条款联网及处理信息，不能把本应用的行为说明理解为整台电脑不会联网。
只有你自行发邮件时，发布者才收到你提供的邮箱、文字及附件，用于支持或隐私请求。该邮箱由 Gmail 托管，邮件的传输、存储适用其服务规则。请只提交必要信息，不发送音频、密码、配对身份文件、完整家庭网络资料或旧原始日志。支持资料仅在处理请求和依法必要的期间保留；你可以通过上述邮箱申请查阅或删除自己主动提交的资料，依法必须保留的情况除外。本政策不承诺应用替你控制第三方系统或他人已收到的副本。

7. 清除、退出和卸载
在应用内先停止投送并断开连接，再选择“清除已保存设备信息”：将关闭自动投送，清除上次设备、各设备偏好及选择的声音输出标识，保留通用音量、模式和语言。取消确认不会清除。网络重新发现的设备仍可能显示，不代表保留旧历史。
如需清理日志、诊断或全部设置：先从托盘彻底退出，按上述路径备份需要的资料，再手动删除选定文件；删除 settings.json 后重启使用默认偏好。若需清除语言等 WebView 本地数据，还须清理对应应用 WebView 用户数据。卸载不保证清除已有日志、导出诊断和所有 WebView 数据；请另在 Windows 启动应用中关闭自启动。
以上为逻辑路径。MSIX 可能把部分路径重定向到本包用户数据目录，传统安装和 MSIX 不保证自动共用设置。导出时界面会显示实际保存位置。不同安装方式和 Windows 版本的数据保留行为可能不同，请在卸载前备份需要的资料。

8. 政策与请求
如实际数据行为有重大变化，应相应更新政策及需要的提示/同意。隐私问题、支持或资料请求请发送到 woyaotingspotify@gmail.com。本政策不排除适用法律赋予你的权利。政策原文：https://github.com/11thStKidzz/DeskAudio-Cast/blob/main/legal/PRIVACY.txt 。

DeskAudio Cast Privacy Policy
Application version: 0.1.13. Updated: 28 October 2026.
Publisher: 11thStKidzz
Privacy and support contact: woyaotingspotify@gmail.com

1. Scope
This policy explains the information this application accesses, processes, transmits and retains. The absence of a server upload does not mean that no personal information is processed. System audio can contain private content, and device names, network addresses and identifiers can relate to people or households. This policy does not replace the policies of Windows, Microsoft Store, WebView2 or an email service you choose to use.

2. System audio
When you start sending, or explicitly enable automatic sending, the application uses Windows WASAPI loopback to read audio playing through the selected output device, including possible notifications or private content. Audio is processed in memory in the application's transport format, ALAC-encoded and sent over the network to your selected receiver. The application does not actively capture the microphone, save captured raw or encoded audio to files, or operate its own cloud audio-upload service. Stop sending to stop this audio transmission. Transmit only content you are entitled to play and share.

3. Discovery, networking and pairing
The application discovers devices using mDNS on reachable networks. It processes device names, IP addresses, ports, device identifiers and protocol capabilities, and exchanges connection, control and playback data with the selected receiver. Identifiers may originate from MAC-form identifiers advertised by a device; they are not used for web browsing or advertising tracking.
Connection validation currently permits private/link-local addresses and globally routable IPv6 addresses obtained through discovery. Manual entry does not accept public addresses. Private addresses may route through a VPN; the application cannot guarantee that every accepted address is on the same physical LAN. Use trusted networks and receivers only. Protocol encryption is not a guarantee against every attack. Current pairing identities are held in memory only, and plaintext persistent-identity entry points are disabled. Identity files created by older standalone tools are not automatically deleted.

4. Local preferences and automatic features
To restore preferences, the application stores the previous receiver's name, address, port and identifier, per-device preferences, the selected Windows output identifier, volume, mode, language and feature switches at the logical path %APPDATA%/com.personal.desktopaudiotransmitter/settings.json. Language is also stored in the application's WebView local storage. Preferences have no automatic expiry and remain until you clear or change them.
Startup and automatic sending are optional and off by default. With automatic sending enabled, background discovery looks for the previous receiver and may transmit all sounds currently playing on the system, using the saved volume and mode. You can turn this off. After a manual stop, the current run will not automatically resume unless you re-enable the option. Windows startup-app controls also govern startup behavior.

5. Logs and diagnostics you export
Current logs allow numeric performance metrics only, not audio samples, encoded bytes, device names, IP addresses, pairing keys or arbitrary raw errors. The logical log directory is %APPDATA%/ConexionAirPlay/logs; its legacy name is retained for compatibility.
At startup and every six hours, the application makes a best-effort attempt to prune its own daily old logs, targeting 14 days and at most 14 files. Recently touched files from the past 24 hours and locked files are skipped, so the target can temporarily be exceeded. Directory links are not followed and unrelated files are not deleted. Logs from earlier versions may contain sensitive content.
Diagnostic JSON files you explicitly export use the logical path %LOCALAPPDATA%/com.personal.desktopaudiotransmitter/diagnostics. They contain the app version, build fingerprint, session status, counters and timings, including up to the latest 64 numeric underrun events per output and fallback-decision information. They do not attach raw logs or contain audio, device names, addresses or keys. Events stay in memory before export. Exported files remain until you delete them and are not removed by log pruning. Exporting does not upload anything; review a file before sharing it.

6. Information received by the publisher and third parties
The application has no application account, advertising or automatic business-telemetry service, and does not automatically email support. Audio and necessary control data go to your selected receiver. Windows, WebView2 and Microsoft Store may connect and process information under their own terms. This application-level description does not mean that your whole computer never connects to the Internet.
Only if you choose to email support does the publisher receive the email address, message and attachments you provide, for support or privacy requests. Gmail hosts this mailbox; email transport and storage are also subject to its service rules. Send only necessary information, not audio, passwords, pairing identity files, complete home-network details or old raw logs. Support information is retained for handling the request and legally necessary periods. You may request access to or deletion of information you voluntarily submitted by contacting the address above, except where retention is required by law. This policy does not promise that the application controls third-party systems or copies already received by others.

7. Clearing information, exit and uninstall
Stop sending and disconnect before using Clear saved device information. This turns off automatic sending and removes the previous receiver, per-device preferences and selected audio-output identifier, while retaining general volume, mode and language preferences. Cancelling confirmation does not clear the data. Devices rediscovered on the network can still appear; that does not indicate retained history.
To remove logs, diagnostics or all settings, fully quit from the tray first, back up information you need, then manually remove selected files at the paths above. Removing settings.json resets preferences on the next launch. Clearing WebView language/local data also requires removing the corresponding application WebView user data. Uninstall does not guarantee removal of existing logs, exported diagnostics or all WebView data. Disable startup in Windows startup-app settings as appropriate.
These are logical paths. MSIX may redirect some paths into package-specific user storage; traditional and MSIX installations are not guaranteed to share settings automatically. The export UI displays the actual saved location. Data retention can vary with the installation method and Windows version. Back up information you need before uninstalling.
